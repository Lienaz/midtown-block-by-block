Felony assaults in Midtown have more than doubled since 2019

The short version

  • Felony assaults in Midtown more than doubled from 2019 to 2025, from 375 to 884, rising about three times as fast as in the city as a whole (+136% vs. +44%).
  • Three in four happen in public, on sidewalks, in subway stations and in stores, mostly from late afternoon into the night.
  • It isn’t just assaults on police, in shelters or in stores: take those out and the count still roughly doubled.
  • Less serious assaults are up too (+43%), and felony assaults are still rising in 2026 (+6.4% through Oct. 18), even as robbery and overall major crime fall.
